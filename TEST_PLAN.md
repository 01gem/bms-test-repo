# BarangayLink — UAT Tester Brief

## Before you begin
- Use the account issued to you. Do not create new accounts.
- The system is being evaluated; some features are intentionally gated.

## What is intentionally limited

- **AI generation is rate-limited** to 200 households per hour per official.
  If you see a message about an hourly budget, that is working as designed —
  wait and try again, or note it and continue with other tasks.
- **SMS notifications are disabled in this build** unless the test coordinator
  has enabled them. The buttons appear but are greyed out.
- **Generated documents carry a fixed signatory** (Hon. Erwin Astronomo,
  Barangay Captain). This is a placeholder for the demo build.
- **The landing page shows sample statistics** for request fulfillment rate
  and average response time. These are placeholders until real history exists.

## What to report

For each screen you test, note:
- Whether the task completed
- Anything that made you stop and think
- Anything that looked broken but turned out not to be

Report defects with the screen name, what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
